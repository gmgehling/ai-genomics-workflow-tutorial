Intelligence for Quantitative Data Analysis in Nursing Research:
A Practical Workflow for Ethical Collaboration. *Biological Research
for Nursing*. Submitted 2026.

This guide was adapted from a presentation delivered at the International Society 
of Nurses in Genetics (ISONG) Annual Meeting. Originally presented as: 
Heinsberg LW. *Partnering with AI for Genomic Informatics: Practical Workflows 
for Data Analysis and Interpretation.* Expert Lecture; International Society of 
Nurses in Genetics; November 2025; Montreal, Canada.

## Overview
This repository is a companion to the manuscript above. The purpose of
the paper was to propose a two-phase workflow for responsible human-AI
collaboration in quantitative data analysis in nursing research. This
repository walks through the synthetic dataset, annotated R code, and
prompt-and-response examples referenced in the manuscript, illustrating
the workflow as a demonstration of the process rather than as a
standalone executable tool.
